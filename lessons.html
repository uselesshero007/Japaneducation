Etiquette & Real Tokyo Practices

Always accompany “ありがとうございます” with a gentle 30-degree bow (eshaku or keirei). Keep your back straight and pause momentarily at the lowest point. This transmits genuine respect and humility in both academia and corporate life in Japan.

lightbulb Bonus: Shortened informal version for friends is just “ありがとう” (Arigatou).
